01 — Design principles

Three ideas move through every lift we build.

間

Ma — negative space

A platform should feel like an absence, not an object. We keep housings shallow and edges honest so a lift disappears into a doorway or a deck until the moment it's needed.

渋い

Shibui — understated form

Nothing is decorated for its own sake. Every seam, rail, and fastener is left visible and unapologetic, finished plainly rather than dressed up.

改善

Kaizen — small corrections

Each model is a revision of the last: a quieter motor, a shorter pit depth, a smoother approach ramp. Improvement in increments, not in leaps.
